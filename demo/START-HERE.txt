FUTURESCAN — JUDGING DEMO

1. Download FutureScan.html and open it in a normal browser (Chrome or Edge
   recommended). Open it as a webpage, not inside a document previewer.
   No internet, installation, account, or API key is required.

2. Fill in the blank form with fictional information, or choose
   "Try a demo profile" at the top right. On mobile, use the sparkle button.

   ANANYA: Prepared planner — 97/100, Ready to plan.
   KABIR: Early explorer — 69/100, Needs preparation.
   RIYA: Ambitious dreamer — 64/100, Pause & verify affordability.

3. Walk through the form. All sample answers are editable. The myth-busters
   are optional. Click "Reveal my FatehScore", or use "See my scorecard"
   once all required details are filled in.

4. Show the four-factor breakdown, sample funding gap, and personal roadmap.
   Use "Explore a what-if" to demonstrate how funding changes the score.
   Part-time work reliance continues to trigger the affordability pathway
   until that answer is changed too.

5. Click "Save scorecard" and choose "Save as PDF". Use A4 paper, default
   scale, and disable browser headers and footers for the cleanest result.
   The Action Desk lets you add a next-step preference to the roadmap;
   it does not create an actual appointment.

Deck weights: Academic fit 30%, Budget preparedness 30%, Documentation 20%,
Timeline readiness 20%. Numerical scoring rules, sample costs, and grade
benchmarks are prototype assumptions. Read "How it works" for details.
This is a planning-readiness demonstration, not an admission or visa predictor.

Answers stay in the open tab. Reloading clears them. Save the scorecard
before closing the browser if you want to keep it.

For developers: FutureScan-source.zip includes the React/Vite source,
scoring model, automated tests, README, and the standalone production HTML.
